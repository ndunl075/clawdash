# Original content and certification

All geometry and motif sequences in this package were authored for Clawdash. No third-party level data, sprites or music is included here. The host synthesizes the named audio patches offline.

Physics `clawdash-physics-1` locks 60Hz, 4px/tick, 28px body, 0.65px/tick² gravity and 11px/tick jump. Input presses buffer for three ticks; grounded edge departure has a short coyote margin; holding jump chains jumps. Simulation positions and velocities are integer thousandths of a pixel. Public snapshots and serialized geometry use pixels and upward altitude. Platform `y` is its top; block `y` is its bottom. Chunk obstacle coordinates are absolute world coordinates.

At 120 BPM, 120px is one beat. Each eight-beat motif spans 960px / 240 ticks. The catalog declares neutral grounded entry/exit, safe approach/departure distances, terrain, difficulty cost, cooldown and exact tick witnesses. Press ticks are zero-based: press 72 is applied to state tick 72. Releases apply on every other tick. The ordinary body stays axis aligned regardless of cosmetic rotation. Spikes use a slightly inset triangle and continuous swept SAT. Solid top/underside contacts precede lethal side contacts, then spike contacts. Spatial buckets and bytewise stable obstacle ordering bound per-tick work.

First Light (36 seconds) introduces single spikes, doubles, a gap and a safe raised surface, separated by recovery sections. Overclock (44 seconds) combines pairs, gaps, raised surfaces and blocks. Night Shift (52 seconds) adds repeated paired-spike and twin-gap phrases, with a central recovery section. Their authored difficulty labels describe intended progression and must be revisited after human playtesting. Horizontal speed stays constant; music texture and obstacle cadence provide escalation without changing physics.

Certification replays the entire baked level from its initial state through completion. Geometry, catalog, physics, beat grid, boundaries and finish must match before a catalog witness is accepted. Tests cover every ordered motif pair and 120 deterministic multi-section compositions. Portable golden fixtures hash every tick, including airborne positions. These checks prove existence of a valid input sequence under the shipping engine. They do **not** prove enjoyment, accessibility, difficulty or game feel.

**Human playtesting remains an uncompleted release gate.** No human gameplay review or clean-machine packaging review has been performed by this package's authoring tests. Before release, play all three tracks manually; record first-attempt readability, retries, gap affordances, audio alignment, small-window visibility and keyboard/touch feel. Deliberate physics changes require a new physics version and recertification; motif/geometry changes require new content IDs and catalog version.
